# **S.A.G.E. Framework™**

A framework for building unique, category-defining offers that are **felt, not just understood**.

---

## **What S.A.G.E. Does**

Most offers compete by adding more features, more promises, or more information.

S.A.G.E. helps you do something different:

* Stand apart from familiar market narratives  
* Reframe the problem through a new lens  
* Create intrigue before explanation  
* Connect with identity and emotion, not just logic

The result is an offer that feels distinctive, memorable, and difficult to compare directly with alternatives.

---

# **S — Subtract the Familiar**

Remove the assumptions, clichés, and industry-standard approaches that everyone else is using.

### **Questions to Ask**

* What is everyone in this market saying?  
* What beliefs have become accepted without being challenged?  
* Which features, promises, or explanations sound interchangeable?

### **Goal**

Create space for a fresh perspective by refusing to repeat what everyone already knows.

### **Example**

Instead of:

"Grow faster with better marketing."

Try:

"Most businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a positioning problem."

---

# **A — Add a New Lens**

Introduce a different way of seeing the problem.

This is the reframe.

### **Questions to Ask**

* What is the real issue beneath the obvious issue?  
* What hidden cause is being ignored?  
* What perspective changes everything?

### **Goal**

Help people see their situation differently.

### **Example**

Instead of:

"You need more productivity."

Try:

"You don't need more productivity. You need fewer unnecessary decisions."

---

# **G — Generate Curiosity, Not Just Clarity**

Clarity helps understanding.

Curiosity creates attention.

Great positioning often makes people lean in before they fully understand.

### **Questions to Ask**

* What surprising statement could challenge assumptions?  
* What idea creates a knowledge gap?  
* What would make someone say, "I've never thought about it that way"?

### **Goal**

Increase engagement through intrigue.

### **Example**

Instead of:

"We help creators build audiences."

Try:

"The fastest-growing creators aren't creating more content."

---

# **E — Elevate With Emotion & Identity**

People rarely buy because of logic alone.

They buy because of who they want to become.

### **Questions to Ask**

* What identity transformation does this create?  
* What emotional outcome matters most?  
* How does success feel, not just look?

### **Goal**

Anchor the offer in self-image, aspiration, and meaning.

### **Example**

Instead of:

"You'll learn how to write better."

Try:

"Become the kind of thinker whose ideas are impossible to ignore."

---

# **The S.A.G.E. Flow**

1. **Subtract the Familiar**  
2. **Add a New Lens**  
3. **Generate Curiosity**  
4. **Elevate With Emotion & Identity**

Formula:

Familiar Belief → New Lens → Curiosity Gap → Identity Shift

---

# **Example**

### **Ordinary Offer**

"We teach entrepreneurs how to grow their businesses."

### **S.A.G.E. Version**

**Subtract the Familiar**

Business growth isn't primarily about working harder.

**Add a New Lens**

Most businesses stall because they blend in.

**Generate Curiosity**

The biggest growth opportunities often appear after you stop competing.

**Elevate With Emotion & Identity**

Become the business people compare others against.

---

## **Core Principle**

People remember what is different.

They buy what feels true.

S.A.G.E. helps create offers that achieve both.
